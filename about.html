01 · A Letter

From the kitchen that
started it all.

We started with one burner, one recipe, and a small brass pot that never seemed to stop simmering. There was no plan beyond getting through the first weekend — and yet here we are, twelve years later, still pouring the same cup.

What began as a table for two became a table for the whole street. Students, grandmothers, night-shift nurses, first dates that turned into marriages — they all found the same corner, and somehow the chai always tasted better when shared.

We have grown since, but slowly. A second kettle. A small oven. A window that opens onto the spice lane. Every addition has been careful, and every recipe has stayed exactly where it began — in the little notebook we still keep behind the counter.

— Amara & Rohan Devi

05 · What We Stand For

Three rules we have
never once broken.

I

Never rush the brew

Seven minutes on a low flame. Every order, every day. If the kitchen is slow, the kettle is not — it is doing its job.

II

Know the source

Every leaf, every pod, every thread of saffron has a name and a face behind it. If we can't visit the garden, we don't buy the crop.

III

Poured, not pushed

No upsell, no upsizing, no rush at the counter. You take the seat you want for as long as you want it — and the cup stays warm.
