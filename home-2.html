03 — Why Chai Haven

Three things we refuse
to compromise on.

01 — Whole Leaf

Sourced from small family gardens.

Single-estate Assam, Darjeeling and Nilgiri leaves arrive whole, never fannings, never dust. The difference is in the depth of the cup.

02 — Slow Craft

Simmered, never rushed.

Small brass kettles, low flames, and a timer we actually watch. The spices bloom slowly — and you can taste the patience.

03 — Made With Care

Family recipes, poured fresh.

Three generations of tweaks and one stubborn rule: every cup is made to order. No pre-mixed, no reheated, no exceptions.

04 — The Craft

Four careful steps,
from leaf to cup.

  1. Step 01

    Grind & Measure

    Whole spices are cracked fresh every morning and measured to the gram — because a pinch too much of clove changes everything.

  2. Step 02

    Simmer Slowly

    Leaves and spice bloom together over a low flame until the whole room smells like a warm afternoon. Roughly seven minutes — never less.

  3. Step 03

    Pour & Froth

    The classic pull — poured high and low from a height until a soft, golden foam settles on top. This is where the chai gets its soul.

  4. Step 04

    Serve Warm

    Handed over while it's still steaming, with a smile and a small biscuit on the side. The last step is the easiest — and the best.
