Our Story

A small stall that grew into a second home

Chai Haven began as a two-burner stall on a busy corner, serving one recipe and one promise: never rush the brew. Word travelled fast, and the queue never really went away.

Today we still grind our own spice mix every morning, still bake in small batches, and still greet every guest like they're walking into our living room.

The Ritual

Four steps to the perfect cup

No machines, no shortcuts — just a kettle, a timer and a good deal of patience.

01

Grind & Measure

Whole spices are cracked fresh each morning and measured to the gram for balance.

02

Simmer Slowly

Leaves and spice bloom together over a low flame until the aroma fills the room.

03

Pour & Froth

The classic pull — poured high and low until a soft, golden foam settles on top.

04

Serve Warm

Handed over while it's still steaming, with a smile and a biscuit on the side.
