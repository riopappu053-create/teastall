02 — The Brewing Ritual

Five slow steps
to a perfect cup.

01

Toast

Whole spices dry-toasted over a low flame until the kitchen fills with warmth.

02

Crush

A stone mortar releases the oils — never a grinder, which heats and dulls them.

03

Steep

Loose-leaf Assam meets the water first — four minutes at exactly 96°C.

04

Simmer

Milk joins the pot, brought up slowly — never boiled twice, never rushed.

05

Pour

The signature pull — a slow stretch from height to aerate and fold the flavours.

04 — From Garden to Cup

A short story
about a long journey.

Every leaf we brew is traceable back to a single garden, a single season, and a single pair of hands. We visit each of our partner estates at least once a year — not to negotiate, but to taste the soil.

The spices travel separately. Cardamom from the Western Ghats, ginger from the highlands of Sikkim, cinnamon from a small cooperative in Ceylon. Only when they are all in the same room do we begin to blend.

06 — The Chai Haven Promise

Four things we
will never compromise.

Freshly Ground

Spices are crushed the morning they're used. Never pre-mixed, never stored for tomorrow.

Fairly Sourced

Every garden is paid above market rate, every season — no exception, no negotiation.

Slowly Brewed

No shortcuts, no pressure cookers. Each pot takes the time it takes — and we wait.

Poured by Hand

Every cup is finished by a person, never a machine. The pull is part of the recipe.
