05 — Frequently Asked

Answers to a few
we hear often.

Do you take reservations?

Yes, for parties of six or more. Smaller tables are kept open for walk-ins, so we recommend arriving a little before peak hours (12–2 PM and 5–7 PM) if you're a group.

Can I order online or for pickup?

Absolutely. Visit the menu page to place a pickup order, or call us directly. Bulk chai for offices and events is available with 24 hours' notice.

Do you have vegan or dairy-free options?

Every signature chai can be made with oat, almond or soy milk at no extra cost. Our pastries rotate daily and always include at least two plant-based options.

Is the tea house accessible?

Yes — step-free entrance, wide aisles and a fully accessible restroom. Let us know about any specific needs when you book and we'll have a table ready.

Do you cater private events?

We host small gatherings up to thirty guests after hours. Chai flights, spice tastings and a custom menu are all part of the package. Drop us a note to plan.
